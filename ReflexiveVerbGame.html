How to Play

Reflexive Verbs

Reflexive verbs are verbs where the subject performs an action on itself. They always end in "-se" in their infinitive form.

Example: afeitarse (to shave oneself)

Conjugation Rules

  • Remove the -se from the infinitive
  • Add the appropriate reflexive pronoun (me, te, se, nos, os, se)
  • Conjugate the remaining verb as normal

Example: yo + afeitarse = me me afeito

Keyboard Shortcuts
