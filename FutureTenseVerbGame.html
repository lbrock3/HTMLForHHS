How to Play

Future Tense

The future tense is used to talk about actions that will happen in the future.

Regular verbs keep their infinitive and add: -é, -ás, -á, -emos, -án

Irregular Stems

  • Drop letter: saber → sabr-, haber → habr-, poder → podr-, querer → querr-
  • Change to -dr-: poner → pondr-, salir → saldr-, tener → tendr-, venir → vendr-
  • Unique: decir → dir-, hacer → har-

Keyboard Shortcuts

Alt + vowel for accents (á, é, í, ó, ú)
