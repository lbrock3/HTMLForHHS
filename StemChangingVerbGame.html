How to Play - Stem-Changing Verbs

Basic Rules

  • Conjugate the verb in the present tense
  • Pay attention to the stem change type shown
  • Remember: No stem changes in nosotros/nosotras forms!

Stem Changes

  • e → ie: pensar → pienso
  • o → ue: dormir → duermo
  • e → i: pedir → pido
  • u → ue: jugar → juego
